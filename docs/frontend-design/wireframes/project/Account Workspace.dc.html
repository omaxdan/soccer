Phase J · Account workspace · spec

Source check: the project has no account, user, session, subscription, billing or entitlement payloads. The only established facts are: sign-up collects email + password (B5), sign-in has a "Forgot password?" entry (B4), the plan names are Guest / Free / Pro / Elite, Subscription Success can show confirmed or pending (B7), and the Phase A header has a signed-in account menu. Everything else here is structure with its dependency named.

AreaShown · depends on
1–2 Shell and headerPhase A header in its signed-in state, with Account reached from the account menu (1d). Workspace header: eyebrow ACCOUNT, h1 Account, "Signed in as {email}". No avatar or profile banner.
3 OverviewAccount details (Email only), a subscription summary (Plan and Subscription status as two separate facts), next steps (links, not actions), and Session / Sign out.
4 SubscriptionCurrent plan: Plan · Subscription status (a word + glyph, as reported, never inferred from the plan) · Renewal / end date ("Not supplied" until the contract provides it). Then Billing, then Plans → Pricing. No history section, because no history source exists.
5 BillingDefault is ◌ NOT AVAILABLE (the product doesn't expose it). Also designed: NO BILLING RECORDS (empty returned) and error + Try again. "Available" is a layout of unlabelled slots only: no amounts, cards, invoices or provider. There's no payment-method section.
6 SecurityPassword: a "Reset password" link that hands off to the B4 reset entry. No password rules, no change-password form, no verification, no MFA. Session: Sign out.
7–8 ActionsSign out (a quiet secondary button, in its own Session panel, never styled as destructive). "See plans" is plain navigation to Pricing. There are no Change plan, Cancel, Resume or Delete account controls: none is supported.
9–13 StatesLoading uses panel skeletons under a live header. Account/session failure is a page-level panel with Try again · Sign in. Everything else fails per section (1h). Sign-out has processing, success and failure (1i). Pending renders only when the contract reports it, with the B7 wording.
20 Three meaningsNO DATA = solid tag + "There's no … on this account." NOT AVAILABLE = dashed ◌ tag + "aren't shown in your account yet." ERROR = "!" + "couldn't be loaded" + Try again.
14–16 LayoutDesktop: 200px vertical section nav (amber left rule) + a content column capped at 720. Tablet: 168px nav. Mobile: a 3-way segmented nav (44px), stacked panels, key/value rows stacked, and Sign out last on the page, away from other controls.
17 Accessibilitynav with aria-current; h1 → h2 per panel; dl for facts; status as text + glyph; errors role=alert; sign-out aria-busy with a live region; amber focus rings; 44px targets.
18–20 FlowPricing → Checkout (B6) → Subscription Success (B7) → Account / Subscription. B7's Continue should land here. Account → Pricing via "See plans"; Checkout is only reached from Pricing and isn't duplicated here.
21 AdminNo admin, provider, pipeline, registry or user-management concepts. The header menu's existing items are unchanged.

Backend dependencies (PENDING): an account contract (identity fields), a subscription contract (plan, status vocabulary, dates), a billing contract, the session / sign-out contract, the password-reset flow, and whether any subscription change or cancellation is offered. When each lands, its section switches from EXAMPLE or NOT AVAILABLE to real values without layout changes.

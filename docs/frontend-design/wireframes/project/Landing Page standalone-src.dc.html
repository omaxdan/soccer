1 · Concept

The page reads like the product: calm dark canvas, one amber action, real PitchTerminal UI in the hero. It moves from what it is (hero) to what you can read (capabilities) to how it's built (flow) to why you can trust it (evidence), then who it serves, how access works, and one final push into the fixtures. Section surfaces alternate canvas → panel band → canvas to create rhythm without new colours.

Desktop 1a · Tablet 1b · Mobile 1c. The same component set is used at all three widths.

9 · Content hierarchy

H1 (1) → H2 per section (7) → H3 for capability, step, tier (13). Body copy is at most two sentences per block. Section eyebrows: Football intelligence · What you get · How it works · Evidence first · Access.

10 · CTA flow

  1. Explore fixtures (primary, hero + final) → Fixtures index. Phase C, open to anonymous users.
  2. How it works (secondary) → scrolls to the in-page flow section. It doesn't link to the unbuilt Features page; it can be pointed at B2 once that exists.
  3. View pricing (bridge + final secondary) → Pricing, B3.
  4. Get access / Sign in (header) → B5 / B4.

Only one amber button per viewport, except in the final CTA band.

11 · Responsive rules

  • Desktop: 1152 rail; hero 5/7; capabilities 3-up; flow 5 across with → arrows; evidence 5/7; audience 4-up; pricing 1.3/1/1; footer brand + 4 columns.
  • Tablet: hero stacks with the full-width preview under the CTAs; capabilities stack one per row; supporting rows keep a 200px title column; flow becomes a vertical list with ↓; audience and pricing 2-up; footer 4 columns under the brand.
  • Mobile: header gets the nav as a second row; the preview keeps 3 fixture rows around the selected match; CTAs fill the width; every grid becomes one column; final CTA is left-aligned with full-width buttons; footer 2-up.
  • Type steps down through fixed min/max bounds (H1 54 → 34, H2 32 → 24); body stays 14–16 at every width.

12 · Accessibility

  • One h1; each section has an h2 and aria-labelledby; capabilities, steps and tiers use h3; principles use a dl; steps use an ol.
  • CTAs are links (they navigate), with a 2px amber focus ring from Phase A; 44px minimum, 48px in the final band.
  • Form badges carry the full word in aria-label; FT uses text and a glyph; SUPPORTS/CONTRADICTS are words, not colours; the selected preview row adds weight and an edge, not colour alone.
  • The preview is a figure with a figcaption that says it shows real recorded data; monograms are aria-hidden.
  • Arrows in the flow are aria-hidden; the order comes from the ol.
  • Contrast follows the verified text tiers; no text is placed on amber except --ink.

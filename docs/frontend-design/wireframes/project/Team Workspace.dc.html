4–5 · Overview and integrated readings

  • Current readings: three reading cards (Phase A ReadingCard) with the verdict text shown verbatim:
    • Readiness: NEUTRAL, "Steady form: last-five points minus prior-five is -7.", evidence Momentum −7, 10 matches.
    • Home and away: NEUTRAL, verdict verbatim, evidence away win rate 66.67 and home win rate 50, 3 matches, Brasileirão only.
    • Consistency: MEASURED, verdict verbatim (includes "Higher is less consistent."), goal-margin volatility 1.89, 10 matches, all competitions.
    Statuses are shown as the words the API supplies. Strength and confidence are null, so they're not shown.
  • Team attributes: Strengths (7 result-based + 1 statistical), Tendencies (2), Weaknesses (1). Columns: label · value (as supplied) · rank n / 20 · league median · level (TOP QUARTILE / BOTTOM QUARTILE). The "better" orientation line comes from qualityOrientation (e.g. "lower is better" for goals conceded); the UI never assumes it.
  • Recent results: last 5 across all competitions (fixtures.recent), with a W/D/L badge from observations.result. The full home/away split lives in Performance.
  • Rail: Competitions this season (participation: Brasileirão 25 of 28, 3 scheduled · Libertadores 1 of 1 · Copa do Brasil 2 of 2) and Unavailable players (availability: Jefté · Meniscus Injury, Paulinho · Muscle Injury, since 17 Sep; no return date recorded).

6 · Squad

  • 29 registered players (squad), joined with player-observations (in squad, started, bench, minutes, goals), valuations (€, as of 17 Sep) and availability (▲ INJURY chip with reason as a tooltip / inline on mobile).
  • Sorted by minutes. No position grouping, numbers, ages or nationality: none are supplied. The page says so.
  • A collapsed "Also appeared this season, not currently registered · 12" lists players present in player-observations but not in the registered squad.
  • — marks values that aren't recorded (e.g. goals where the statistic is absent). The player name links to Player (G).

7–8 · Performance and home/away

  • Performance indicators (performance.overall + byCompetition):
    • Home form 48.33 index (6 matches)
    • Away form 34 index (4 matches, flagged "small sample" because meetsThreshold is false)
    • Momentum −7 pts
    • Goal-margin volatility 1.89 goals ("size only, no direction")
    • Giant-killer points per match 1.33
    • Home win rate 50 and away win rate 66.67 (Brasileirão)
    The direction line reads "higher = stronger" from HIGHER_IS_STRONGER.
  • Recent home / Recent away: two separate lists of 5 each (homeAwayContext) with H / A tags, so venue context is never mixed.
  • Performance signals: group switcher (Scoring · Conceding · Results · Both scored · Finishing vs xG · Defending vs xGA · Chance creation). The table shows Prev 5 · Last 5 · Season · Home · Away using the API's displayValue, plus the current runs (streaks). Formulas aren't shown.

9 · History

  • Season record (temporal season.results): W16 D8 L4 · 56 pts · GF 50 · GA 24 · +26, 28 matches, all competitions.
  • Match by match: a mirrored bar chart of goals for (up) and against (down) for all 28 observations, oldest → latest, with the W/D/L strip underneath. It has an aria description, and the match log is its data table.
  • Match log: latest first, 10 shown with a Show all 28 option; date · opponent (v / @) · competition · score · xG · xGA · result. Rows link to Match (F). Copa do Brasil matches have no xG, so they show —.
  • Season statistics: possession avg, shots, shots on target, xG, big chances, corners, fouls and yellows, with coverage where it's below 28/28.
  • Earlier seasons: records start 28 Jan 2026, so there's no multi-season view. It's stated, not faked.

Performance answers "now" (last 5 vs previous 5, venue split, indicators). History answers "the whole recorded period" (every match, season totals). No chart appears on both tabs.

15–17 · Layouts

  • Desktop: 1152 rail; Overview 8 / 4; Squad, Performance and History full width, with History's log and stats at 7 / 5.
  • Tablet: rail stacks under main; readings 2-up; the squad keeps its columns, narrower.
  • Mobile: 44px scrolling tabs; readings stacked; attributes drop Median and Level (still reachable via the orientation line and rank); squad as stacked rows (name + status, then minutes · started/bench · goals · value); signals table scrolls horizontally inside its panel; the chart stays full width.

18 · Accessibility

  • h1 team → h2 sections → h3 reading cards; tabs use role=tab; signal groups use a radiogroup.
  • All tabular data uses role=table with column headers; the squad "Squad" header has a title "Matches in the matchday squad".
  • W/D/L badges have titles and words; statuses are words; "small sample" is text, not only colour.
  • The chart has aria-label text and the match log as its table equivalent.
